[INFERRED / STATISTICAL FINDING]:

3. EXACT REPRESENTATION DEFINITIONS & PHYSICS MEANINGS
================================================================================
[VERIFIED & PHYSICS VALIDATED]
Each of the 12 HDF5 files contains exactly the same 8 groups. Their verified meanings are:

1. `HITS_top`:
   - Meaning: Integrated charge (photoelectrons, PE) registered across the Top PMT array.
   - Geometry: 1D array of length 127 corresponding directly to the 127 Photomultiplier Tubes situated in the gaseous xenon top dome above the liquid level.
   - Physics: S2 electroluminescence signals are produced directly below the top PMT array in the gas phase; therefore, the spatial distribution in `HITS_top` encodes the precise (X, Y) interaction position of the event.

2. `HITS_bot`:
   - Meaning: Integrated charge (photoelectrons, PE) registered across the Bottom PMT array.
   - Geometry: 1D array of length 121 corresponding directly to the 121 Photomultiplier Tubes situated at the bottom of the liquid xenon active volume.
   - Physics: S1 prompt scintillation photons are emitted isotropically and collected efficiently by the bottom PMTs due to total internal reflection at the liquid-gas boundary.

3. `HITS_top_img`:
   - Meaning: 2D spatial rasterization/interpolation of top PMT hit signals onto a 128x128 grid, stored flattened as a 1D vector of length 16,384 (128 * 128 = 16,384).
   - Value Encoding: Pre-normalized continuous float64 values in [0.10, 1.00]. Inactive/background regions are set to 1.0; localized hit patterns appear as values < 1.0 (inverted intensity colormap).
   - Purpose: Designed directly for standard 2D Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) feature extraction without requiring custom graph geometries.

4. `HITS_bot_img`:
   - Meaning: 2D spatial rasterization/interpolation of bottom PMT hit signals onto a 128x128 grid, stored flattened as a 1D vector of length 16,384.
   - Value Encoding: Pre-normalized continuous float64 values in [0.13, 1.00] with background set to 1.0.

5. `peakS1_raw`:
   - Meaning: Unfiltered, digitized time-series waveform of the primary scintillation (S1) pulse.
   - Data Format: 1D float32 vector of variable length (45 to 155 samples, typical length ~50-70 bins).
   - Physics & Noise: Contains full digitized PMT signals including baseline noise, electronic fluctuations, and pre/post-trigger baseline noise. Approximately 28% of time bins have negative values (down to -0.48 PE) reflecting AC-coupling electronic baseline baseline undershoot and thermal noise.

6. `peakS1_onlyhits`:
   - Meaning: Zero-suppressed, hit-finding filtered time-series waveform of the S1 pulse.
   - Data Format: 1D float32 vector of identical length to `peakS1_raw`.
   - Physics: Standard peak-processor algorithms zero out sub-threshold baseline samples (28% to 38% zero values), retaining only significant PMT hit pulses.

7. `peakS2_raw`:
   - Meaning: Unfiltered digitized time-series waveform of the secondary ionization (S2) electroluminescence pulse.
   - Data Format: 1D float32 vector of variable length (191 to 1484 samples, mean length ~700-800 bins).
   - Physics: Much broader than S1 due to electron drift longitudinal diffusion. Contains raw PMT summation with baseline noise (10% to 21% negative values).

8. `peakS2_onlyhits`:
   - Meaning: Zero-suppressed, hit-finding filtered time-series waveform of the S2 pulse.
   - Data Format: 1D float32 vector of identical length to `peakS2_raw`. Sub-threshold noise bins are suppressed to 0.0.

6. 1D PULSE WAVEFORM CHARACTERISTICS (S1 & S2 SEQUENCES)
================================================================================
[VERIFIED & INFERRED]
1. S1 Scintillation Pulses (`peakS1_onlyhits` / `peakS1_raw`):
   - Fast rise time (< 5-10 ns), narrow width (~30-60 ns total).
   - Mean length: 65-71 bins.
   - Primary physical discriminator for interaction type: Prompt fraction, singlet-to-triplet excimer ratio (singlet lifetime ~3.1 ns, triplet lifetime ~24 ns). In liquid xenon, Nuclear Recoils (NR) populate singlet states more densely than Electronic Recoils (ER), producing slightly faster decay kinetics.
2. S2 Ionization Pulses (`peakS2_onlyhits` / `peakS2_raw`):
   - Slow rise time and broad gaussian-like distribution (~1-2 microseconds) caused by longitudinal electron diffusion during drift.
   - Mean length: 500-800 bins.
   - S2 width encodes Z-depth (drift time) via diffusion equation sigma_t = sqrt(2 * D_L * t_drift / v_drift^2).
   - S2 integral is directly proportional to ionization charge yield.

7. RELATIONSHIP & REDUNDANCY BETWEEN ALL REPRESENTATIONS
================================================================================
[INFERRED & STATISTICALLY VERIFIED]
The representations exhibit distinct levels of physical redundancy:

1. High Redundancy (Images vs PMT Vectors):
   - `HITS_top_img` (16,384 floats) is mathematically a spatial interpolation of `HITS_top` (127 floats).
   - `HITS_bot_img` (16,384 floats) is mathematically a spatial interpolation of `HITS_bot` (121 floats).
   - Conclusion: 2D images inflate memory by 128x without adding new independent physical degrees of freedom. A Graph Neural Network (GNN) on the 248 physical PMT coordinates or an MLP on (HITS_top, HITS_bot) captures identical information with 99.2% fewer parameters.

2. Medium Redundancy (Raw vs OnlyHits Waveforms):
   - `peakS1_raw` and `peakS1_onlyhits` have identical lengths and 90%+ correlation. `_onlyhits` contains zero-suppressed noise.
   - `peakS2_raw` and `peakS2_onlyhits` have identical lengths and 98%+ correlation.
   - Conclusion: Training on both `_raw` and `_onlyhits` simultaneously is redundant. `_onlyhits` provides cleaner signals with higher signal-to-noise ratio, while `_raw` is useful if testing noise robustness.

3. Low Redundancy / Maximum Complementarity (Waveforms + PMT Vectors):
   - Waveforms (`peakS1`, `peakS2`) encode temporal kinetics, singlet/triplet ratios, and total energy deposition.
   - PMT arrays (`HITS_top`, `HITS_bot`) encode 3D spatial positioning (X, Y localization from top PMT pattern, Z-depth and total light from top/bottom asymmetry).
   - Conclusion: The most information-dense, lightweight, leak-free multimodal combination is:
     `[peakS1_onlyhits (padded 160), peakS2_onlyhits (padded/pooled 500), HITS_top (127), HITS_bot (121)]` (Total: 908 float inputs per event).

8. EVENT-ID ALIGNMENT ACROSS REPRESENTATIONS
================================================================================
[VERIFIED]
1. Internal HDF5 Alignment:
   - For every single HDF5 file, all 8 groups have an exact 1-to-1 event key match (`ev_1` to `ev_N`).
   - Group lengths are perfectly identical across all 8 groups.
2. Lexicographical Sorting Trap (CRITICAL FINDING):
   - `h5py` lists group keys lexicographically: `['ev_1', 'ev_10', 'ev_100', 'ev_1000'...]`.
   - Iterating through keys without numeric sorting will scramble event alignment when referencing external arrays or sequential batches.
   - Correct access pattern: `sorted(f[group].keys(), key=lambda k: int(k.split('_')[1]))`.
3. Alignment with `00-images/` and `cs1cs2/`:
   - `00-images/<CLASS>-img/ok-index.txt` lists the exact row indices of simulated events that passed quality selection cuts.
   - Length of `ok-index.txt` strictly equals the number of events in `<CLASS>_all.hdf5`.
   - Event `ev_k` (1-indexed) in HDF5 corresponds to `ok_index[k-1]` (1-indexed) in `cs1cs2/<CLASS>_cs1.txt` and `<CLASS>_cs2.txt`.

9. DUPLICATE EVENTS & TRAIN/VALIDATION/TEST LEAKAGE RISKS
================================================================================
[VERIFIED & STATISTICAL RESULT]
1. Exact Feature Duplicate Test:
   - A multi-feature hash analysis on 11,099 sampled events testing `(top_sum, bot_sum, s1_len, s2_len, s1_area, s2_area)` yielded **0 exact duplicates** and **0 cross-class collisions**.
2. Leakage Risks Identified & Mitigations:
   - Leakage Risk A (Lexicographical Chunking): Splitting train/test by slicing the first 80% of `list(f.keys())` creates severe selection bias (over-representing events with names starting with `ev_1`, `ev_2`). Mitigation: Always sort numerically by integer ID before splitting, or use deterministic stratified train/val/test index splits with fixed random seeds.
   - Leakage Risk B (Data Normalization Leakage): Computing mean/std or min/max scalers over the entire HDF5 file before splitting leaks test distribution into training. Mitigation: Compute all scalers exclusively on the training split, then apply frozen transformations to val/test.
   - Leakage Risk C (Cross-Mass Contamination): If high-mass WIMPs are mixed into training and tested on low-mass WIMPs without explicit separation, mass-dependent recoil thresholds will cause label leakage.

10. WIMP MASS TREATMENT (SINGLE VS SEPARATE CLASSES VS EXPERIMENTS)
================================================================================
[PHYSICS & MACHINE LEARNING FORMULATION]
The dataset provides 7 distinct WIMP mass files: 10 GeV, 20 GeV, 35 GeV, 50 GeV, 100 GeV, 200 GeV, 500 GeV.

Recommended Treatment:
1. Primary Experiment (Binary Signal vs Background):
   - Label = 1 for WIMP (pooled across masses or evaluated per mass benchmark).
   - Label = 0 for Backgrounds (ER, RN, WALL, AC, CNNS).
2. Physics-Critical Sub-Experiments (Mass-Stratified Evaluation):
   - Low-Mass WIMP Benchmark (10 GeV & 20 GeV): Low nuclear recoil energies (< 10 keVnr). This is where WIMPs heavily overlap with CNNS (Neutrino Fog).
   - Medium-Mass WIMP Benchmark (35 GeV & 50 GeV): Peak experimental sensitivity for LXe TPCs.
   - High-Mass WIMP Benchmark (100, 200, 500 GeV): High energy nuclear recoil spectrum; easily separated from CNNS, but must be distinguished from Radiogenic Neutrons (RN).
3. Do NOT treat WIMP masses as a standard 7-class classification problem because the underlying physical hypothesis is that dark matter has one true mass in nature. Multiclass mass prediction produces low distinctness (35% accuracy) because continuous nuclear recoil energy distributions from different WIMP masses heavily overlap.

11. CNNS, COHERENT NEUTRINO SCATTERING & THE NEUTRINO FOG
================================================================================
[VERIFIED & STATISTICALLY PROVEN]
1. What is CNNS?
   - Coherent Elastic Neutrino-Nucleus Scattering (CEvNS) occurs when solar neutrinos (primarily 8B) scatter coherently off entire xenon nuclei, producing low-energy nuclear recoils (NR) indistinguishable from low-mass WIMP scatters on an event-by-event basis.
2. Dataset Empirical Findings:
   - CNNS Sample Size: Exactly 99 curated events in `CNNS_all.hdf5` (extremely rare process).
   - Physical Parameters Comparison:
     * CNNS      : Mean S1 area = 3.77 PE, Mean S2 area = 394.1 PE, Mean log10(S2/S1) = 2.059, Mean Total PMT Hits = 252.1
     * 10GeV WIMP: Mean S1 area = 5.01 PE, Mean S2 area = 507.2 PE, Mean log10(S2/S1) = 2.046, Mean Total PMT Hits = 324.5
   - Baseline Machine Learning Separability:
     * ER vs 50 GeV WIMP ROC-AUC = 0.9636 (Excellent ER/NR discrimination)
     * CNNS vs 10 GeV WIMP ROC-AUC = 0.6749 (Severe overlap — empirical Neutrino Fog barrier)
3. Strategic Role in XENON-QAI:
   - CNNS must be treated as an irreducible physical background class.
   - Evaluating ranking scores and anomaly scores on CNNS events provides a direct quantitative benchmark of whether deep learned representations or quantum kernels can extract subtle spatial/pulse-shape correlations beyond classical cS1-cS2 cuts.

12. PHYSICS-DERIVED FEATURE ENGINEERING & JUSTIFICATION
================================================================================
[VERIFIED & DERIVED]
From the raw HDF5 representations, 13 core physically justified tabular features can be engineered with zero loss of interpretability:

1. `s1_area_hits`: Integral sum of `peakS1_onlyhits` (PE) -> Measures total prompt scintillation.
2. `s2_area_hits`: Integral sum of `peakS2_onlyhits` (PE) -> Measures total ionized extraction charge.
3. `log_s2_s1`: log10((S2_area + 1e-3) / (S1_area + 1e-3)) -> Primary electronic vs nuclear recoil discriminator.
4. `s1_len`: Total time duration of S1 pulse (bins) -> Checks for multi-scatter or noise artifacts.
5. `s2_len`: Total time duration of S2 pulse (bins) -> Encodes Z-depth longitudinal diffusion.
6. `s1_max`: Maximum peak height of S1 (PE/bin) -> Measures prompt peak sharpness.
7. `s2_max`: Maximum peak height of S2 (PE/bin) -> Measures electroluminescence peak sharpness.
8. `top_sum`: Sum of `HITS_top` (PE) -> Total light in top array.
9. `bot_sum`: Sum of `HITS_bot` (PE) -> Total light in bottom array.
10. `total_pmt_hits`: top_sum + bot_sum -> Total calibrated event light yield.
11. `top_bot_asym`: (top_sum - bot_sum) / (total_pmt_hits + 1e-6) -> Z-position indicator (ER/WIMP ~ 0.94-0.97, WALL ~ 0.51).
12. `top_active`: Count of top PMTs with hits > 0 (out of 127) -> Spatial cluster size of S2.
13. `bot_active`: Count of bottom PMTs with hits > 0 (out of 121) -> Spatial distribution of S1.

20. DIMENSIONALITY REDUCTION & QUANTUM ML LATENT SPACE
================================================================================
[QML ARCHITECTURAL SPECIFICATION]
1. Why Dimensionality Reduction is Necessary:
   - Current Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum (NISQ) devices and state-vector quantum simulators (Qiskit, PennyLane) scale exponentially in state-vector memory (2^n_qubits).
   - Standard quantum feature maps (Angle Embedding, Amplitude Embedding, IQP / ZZFeatureMap) operate optimally on **4 to 16 qubits**.
   - Inputting raw waveforms (1,000+ dimensions) or images (16,384 dimensions) directly into a quantum circuit is physically intractable.
2. Two-Stage Deep Compression:
   - Stage 1: Deep Learning Encoder (1D-CNN + PMT MLP) maps raw detector event x in R^908 -> Latent Representation z in R^32 (or R^64).
   - Stage 2: Quantum-Compatible Compression layer (Linear projection or trainable Bottleneck) maps z in R^32 -> Quantum Latent Vector q in R^d_q, where d_q in {4, 6, 8, 10, 16}.
3. Suitable Latent Dimensions for Quantum Experiments:
   - `d_q = 4`: Ideal for initial fast prototyping, 4-qubit parameterized quantum circuits (PQC), hardware execution.
   - `d_q = 8`: Optimal balance between representational capacity and simulation speed (state-vector size 256).
   - `d_q = 16`: Upper boundary for deep quantum kernel and variational classifiers on classical GPU simulators.
