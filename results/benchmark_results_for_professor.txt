================================================================================
SECTION 1 — EXPERIMENTAL SETUP
================================================================================

- Dataset: Simulated XENON1T dual-phase liquid-xenon time projection chamber (TPC) dataset.
- Event Classes: 12 distinct classes (5 physical backgrounds: ER, RN, WALL, AC, CNNS; 7 WIMP signal masses: 10, 20, 35, 50, 100, 200, 500 GeV).
- Benchmark Scales:
  * Intermediate benchmark: 5,599 events (500 events/class target).
  * Scaling benchmark: 22,099 events (2,000 events/class target).
- Split Methodology: 70% Train / 15% Validation / 15% Test deterministic stratified split (random seed = 42).
- Data Leakage Prevention: Preprocessing transforms and feature scalers fitted strictly on training data only. All splits verified disjoint at event-ID level.
- Pipeline Architecture:
  * 13D domain-engineered physics feature extraction (energy, log10(S2/S1), spatial radii, pulse timing).
  * 13D Classical Baselines (Random Forest, XGBoost, Logistic Regression, MLP).
  * Multimodal Deep 1D-CNN combining S1 waveform (160 bins), S2 waveform (500 bins), Top/Bottom PMT arrays (248 channels), and 13D physics features.
  * Frozen compact latent space representation: z in R^16 extracted after CNN training.
  * Parameter-Matched Classical Head (37 parameters) trained on frozen z.
  * Quantum Variational Classifiers (QNN, 4 qubits) with circuit depths L=2, L=4, L=6 evaluated on the exact same frozen z.
  * QNN execution: Classically simulated on CPU via PennyLane statevector simulation (default.qubit).
  * No architectural or hyperparameter changes between the 5.6K and 22.1K scaling runs.

================================================================================
SECTION 5 — MAIN OBSERVATIONS
================================================================================

1. The Multimodal CNN improved substantially with dataset scale:
   5.6K ROC-AUC = 0.8452 -> 22.1K ROC-AUC = 0.8706 (+0.0254).

2. At 22.1K events, the Multimodal CNN achieved the highest ROC-AUC (0.8706) and PR-AUC (0.8843) among all evaluated models.

3. The 13D MLP remained a strong tabular baseline across scales:
   5.6K ROC-AUC = 0.8644 -> 22.1K ROC-AUC = 0.8675 (+0.0031).

4. The frozen-latent classical head (37 parameters) reached:
   22.1K ROC-AUC = 0.8699, PR-AUC = 0.8822.

5. QNN performance across circuit depths at 22.1K:
   L=2 (16 params) -> ROC-AUC = 0.8662, PR-AUC = 0.8735
   L=4 (32 params) -> ROC-AUC = 0.8609, PR-AUC = 0.8718
   L=6 (48 params) -> ROC-AUC = 0.8556, PR-AUC = 0.8554

6. At the 22.1K scale, L=2 was the strongest of the three QNN configurations by ROC-AUC and PR-AUC.

7. No quantum advantage is claimed.

8. The QNN was evaluated on the exact same frozen latent representation z in R^16 as the classical head.

9. All experiments completed with zero NaN/Inf values, zero runtime failures, and zero memory leaks.
